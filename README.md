# Global Skills Passport (GSP)
### Education Without Borders

**Turning Displaced Talent into Recognised Skills and Global Opportunities**

## About the Project

Global Skills Passport (GSP) is an education and professional development platform designed to empower refugees and displaced people by providing access to quality education, practical skills training, credible certification pathways, and employment opportunities.

Our mission is to ensure that displacement, nationality, financial circumstances, or geographical location do not prevent talented individuals from developing their abilities and demonstrating their professional value.

We believe that talent exists everywhere, but opportunities are not equally distributed.

## The Problem

Millions of displaced people have educational backgrounds, professional experience, and technical skills that remain underutilised because of systemic barriers.

These include:

- Foreign qualifications that are difficult to recognise.
- Missing academic documents and certificates.
- Limited access to affordable professional education.
- Financial and administrative barriers to employment.
- Lack of local work experience and professional networks.
- Limited access to internationally recognised certification pathways.

These challenges contribute to brain waste, where skilled individuals cannot fully contribute to society and the economy.

## Our Solution

Global Skills Passport provides a connected pathway from learning to professional opportunity.

The platform will bring together:

1. **Global Learning Academy:** Accessible professional courses across multiple career pathways.
2. **AI Learning Assistant:** Personalised explanations, learning support and study recommendations.
3. **Practical Skills Assessment:** Projects, assignments and examinations that demonstrate real competencies.
4. **Professional Certification:** Pathways to qualifications issued or endorsed by recognised awarding institutions.
5. **Digital Skills Passport:** A portable professional profile containing verified skills, credentials and project evidence.
6. **Employment Opportunities:** Connections to mentors, internships, employers and professional networks.

## Our Learning Pathways

The platform will initially focus on eight professional areas:

- Software Development
- Artificial Intelligence
- Data Science and Analytics
- Cybersecurity
- Digital Marketing
- Business and Entrepreneurship
- Project Management
- Graphic Design

## How It Works

1. Learners create an account.
2. They select a professional learning pathway.
3. They access structured courses and learning resources.
4. They complete practical projects and assessments.
5. Their competencies are assessed against defined standards.
6. Eligible learners receive credentials through recognised awarding partners.
7. They build their Global Skills Passport and access professional opportunities.

## International Recognition

GSP aims to make qualifications and skills more portable across borders.

We will work with accredited universities, technical institutions, professional certification providers and employers to establish credible learning and assessment pathways.

Certificates issued by GSP itself will clearly distinguish course completion from externally accredited qualifications. International equivalence will only be claimed where an authorised institution or relevant recognition body has established it.
